Model & conventions

Ray vector [height, slope], translation T(d) = [[1,d],[0,1]], thin group L(φ) = [[1,0],[−φ,1]]. For system matrix [A,B;C,D] through G4, EFL = −1/C and fixed-image focus requires A + bC = 0. Eliminating G3 position gives a quadratic in G2 position. Both finite real roots are retained and verified against the system matrix, including roots outside the ordered group interval.

The Petzval estimate uses Σφᵢ/nᵢ and the squared-power sum uses Σφᵢ². These are first-order proxies; no aberration, tolerance, finite-element collision, or real-surface AOI prediction is implied. F/# varies linearly with requested EFL. The pupil is aimed at the selected group; incident slope is measured immediately before each thin group. Root coverage and extrema are sampled, not a proof between samples.
